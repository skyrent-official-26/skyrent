🎁 How rewards work

Once you reach a milestone, your reward becomes available to claim. After claiming, it will remain Pending Approval until reviewed by SkyRent administration.

Once approved, the reward becomes Collected.

Rewards are handled separately from your regular commission and are not automatically added to your available balance.
