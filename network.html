🧭 How Your Commission Takes Off
1
Share Your Referral Share your personal SkyRent referral code or referral link with people you invite to join your network.
2
Your Member Joins The invited member registers through your referral and becomes part of your network.
3
A Plan Is Purchased Referral commission is based on the purchased aircraft plan, not simply on registration.
4
Commission Takes Off Your eligible network level determines whether the commission is 25%, 3% or 1% of the qualifying plan amount.

📋 Commission Guide
Important: The amounts shown on the flight board are examples calculated from each aircraft plan's investment amount using the current A/B/C commission rates.

Level A — Boarding: 25% of the qualifying plan amount.
Level B — Take Off: 3% of the qualifying plan amount.
Level C — Cruising: 1% of the qualifying plan amount.

Referral commissions are generated according to SkyRent's referral rules when a qualifying network member purchases a plan.
