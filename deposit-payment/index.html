📋 How to Make Your Deposit

1. Select a Payment Account
Choose Payment 1 above. Payment 2 is temporarily unavailable. The QR code for the selected account will appear.

2. Scan the QR Code
Open your GCash or Maya app and scan the displayed QR code.

3. Send the Exact Amount
Send exactly ₱0. Do not send a different amount because your payment will be checked against your deposit request.

4. Complete the Payment
Make sure your payment is successfully completed before submitting this deposit request.

5. Select Your Payment Method
Choose GCash if you paid using GCash, or choose Maya if you paid using Maya.

6. Enter Your Reference Number
Copy the payment reference number exactly as shown on your payment receipt and enter it in the field below.

⚠️ Important:
Each payment reference number can only be registered once. Do not use a reference number that has already been submitted by another deposit.

7. Submit Your Deposit
After checking all the information, tap “I Have Completed Payment”.

8. Wait for Admin Verification
Your deposit will initially show as Pending Approval. The admin will verify your payment and reference number before your balance is credited.

9. Check Your Deposit Status
After submitting, you will be redirected to your Deposit History, where you can monitor the status of your deposit.

🔔 Please Note
Your balance will be credited immediately once verified and approved. Please keep your payment receipt until the deposit has been successfully approved.
